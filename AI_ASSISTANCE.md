# AI assistance disclosure

AI assistance was used **for writing and editorial support only**.

It was used to help organize prose, improve readability, identify wording that
could overstate evidence, and maintain consistency across the public disclosure
documents.

AI assistance did not collect live data, operate payment systems, submit
requests to third-party payment infrastructure, generate or alter empirical
observations, determine account status, access private correspondence, or make
final decisions about the evidence and claims. The authors remain responsible
for the study design, evidence handling, source verification, technical claims,
corrections, and every public statement in this repository.

This disclosure applies to the public repository text and is not a substitute
for the separate disclosure requirements of a conference, journal, archive, or
other publication venue.
